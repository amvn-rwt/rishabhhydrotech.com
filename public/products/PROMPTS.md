# Catalogue product image prompts (70)

Workflow for each card:

1. Google the **Search** query below. Open a clear product photo (catalogue / supplier page is fine).
2. Upload that photo into ChatGPT as a reference.
3. Paste **Shared style** + the product prompt. Ask for a polished studio catalogue image matching the reference shape, not a copy of any brand logo or packaging text.
4. Save as PNG at the path shown (~1200×1200, square).

Cards are already wired: missing files stay as grey placeholders until added.

## Shared style (paste first on every generation)

```
Photorealistic industrial product studio photo for a B2B hydraulics catalogue card.
Light cool-grey seamless backdrop (#E8EAED to #F9FAFB). Soft diffused front-left lighting, soft shadows.
Matte cast iron / steel / chrome, brand-new unused part. Centre the product in the frame.
Composition critically important: leave generous empty padding on the LEFT and RIGHT edges (about 12-18% of the image width on each side). The product must not touch or crowd the left or right borders. Top and bottom padding should stay comfortable and balanced, similar to a catalogue card crop. Do not stretch the product edge-to-edge horizontally.
Square 1:1. Product is the clear subject but with breathing room. Sharp focus. No logos, no brand names, no text, no people, no factory, no lifestyle props, no purple tones.
Use the attached photo only as a shape/layout reference. Recreate a clean generic version for our website catalogue. Do not copy brand marks, labels, or packaging text.
```

Aim for ~1200×1200 PNG. One clear product (or small related set) per card, distinct from siblings in the same category.
